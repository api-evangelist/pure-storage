Your AI Strategy Is Stuck Behind Your Application-Centric Architecture by Everpure Blog Summary To scale enterprise AI, organizations must transition from application-centric architectures to an Enterprise Data Cloud that unifies data, automates governance, and delivers data intelligence across the environment. For the last two decades, the application was the center of gravity in the enterprise. It owned the data, decided where it lived, and controlled who got to use it.
